Notes and projects from [DataTalks.Club's AI Dev Tools Zoomcamp](https://datatalks.club/blog/ai-dev-tools-zoomcamp.html), a free course on building software with AI. I'm using the course to explore how coding agents fit into everyday engineering: turning ideas into specifications, breaking work into tasks, and reviewing and testing the results.

The collection follows that workflow through building and deploying a full-stack application, extending agents with the Model Context Protocol (MCP), skills, and plugins, and applying AI tools to security reviews and DevOps. The focus is on practical decisions: what context to provide, how to check generated code, and where human judgment is needed.
