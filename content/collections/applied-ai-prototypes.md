I'm using [Arpit Bhayani's Applied AI course](https://arpitbhayani.me/applied-ai/) and its [public prototypes](https://github.com/arpitbbhayani/prototypes-ai) to study how LLM applications behave: how they use evidence and tools, preserve execution state, manage memory, and respond to failures. The sequence follows the mechanisms and questions rather than the live course's schedule, with mathematical and systems background introduced where it is needed.

Each draft entry contains a lesson scope, a companion-code discussion, and known limitations. Root-relative companion directories `projects/applied-ai-prototypes/aap1` through `aap44` now hold pinned upstream references where available, or guidance explaining a missing implementation. These imports are not completed lesson implementations or reproduced results. Our adaptations, worked explanations and measured experiments will be developed separately as authoring proceeds.
